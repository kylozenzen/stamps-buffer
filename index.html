Built for the part after the post is drafted

Buffer helps you schedule. Stamp helps you get the “looks good” first.

For creators, freelancers, and social media managers who still get approvals through screenshots, email threads, DMs, and the ancient ritual of “just checking in again.”

How it works

From Buffer draft to final stamp in four steps.

01

Connect Buffer

Pull drafts, needs-approval posts, and scheduled posts into one approval board.

02

Assign a client

Drop posts into simple client buckets. No CRM swamp. No spreadsheet cosplay.

03

Share the room

Send one approval room link so the client team can view, comment, or request changes.

04

Stamp approval

The owner uses a private code for final sign-off, giving you a lightweight paper trail.
